Tini's Mac and Cheese

350°F • 25–30 min + 2m broil
Verified Seasoning Blend (split in 2 halves): 1 tsp smoked paprika • 3/4 tsp kosher salt • 1/2 tsp garlic powder • 1/2 tsp black pepper
1
Preheat oven to 350°F and butter a 9×13 baking dish.
2
Shred all 2.5 lbs of block cheese (16 oz mozzarella, 16 oz Colby Jack, 8 oz sharp cheddar). Toss and split into 2 equal bowls.
3
Boil 1 lb cavatappi in heavily salted water for 8 min (7 min if baking later). Drain well.
4
Roux: Melt 3 tbsp butter over medium heat. Bloom half the spices 30 sec, whisk in 3 tbsp flour, cook 2–3 min.
5
Sauce: Stream in 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk, whisk 1 min. Whisk in 1 tbsp Dijon, then 2 cups heavy cream and remaining spices. Simmer 2 min.
6
Turn heat to LOW. Melt in Bowl #1 cheese one handful at a time. Fold in cooked pasta.
7
Layer in dish: Half mac → half Bowl #2 cheese → rest of mac → top with remaining cheese. Bake 25–30 min, broil 2 min. Garnish with chives.

Savory Sausage and Bacon Stuffing

350°F • 35–40 min
1
Cook chopped bacon until crisp; remove to a bowl. Brown 16 oz sage sausage in the same pan, breaking into crumbles.
2
Saute diced celery and 1 yellow onion with butter until soft (6–8 min). Stir in optional soy sauce and poultry seasoning.
3
Combine sausage, bacon, veggies, and herb bread cubes in a large bowl. Pour in chicken broth (about 2.5–3 cups) until moistened but not soggy.
4
Transfer to greased 9×13 dish. Bake covered 20 min at 350°F, then uncovered 15–20 min until crisp on top.

French Green Bean Bake

350°F • 30–35 min
1
Drain 4 cans French style green beans thoroughly. Saute diced yellow onion if using.
2
Whisk 2 cans Cream of Mushroom soup, 1 cup milk, black pepper, and half the crispy fried onions (plus eggs if your recipe uses them). Fold in green beans.
3
Spread into a greased baking dish. Bake 25 min at 350°F, top with remaining crispy fried onions, and bake 5–10 min more until golden.

Orange-Cinnamon Cranberry Sauce

Stovetop • 15 min
1
Zest and juice 2 navel oranges. Add water to reach 1 cup total liquid.
2
Combine liquid, 1 cup light brown sugar, 1–2 cinnamon sticks, and optional bay leaves in a saucepan. Bring to a boil to dissolve sugar.
3
Add 12 oz fresh cranberries and orange zest. Simmer 10–12 min until berries burst and sauce thickens. Remove cinnamon sticks and bay leaves before chilling.
