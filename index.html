Seasoning Blend (mix in small bowl, use in 2 halves): 1 tsp smoked paprika • 1 tsp garlic powder • 1 tsp kosher salt • 1 tsp black pepper
Tap a step to check it off
1
Preheat oven to 350°F and butter a 9×13 baking dish.
2
Shred all 2.5 lbs of cheese (16 oz mozzarella, 16 oz Colby Jack, 8 oz sharp cheddar). Toss together and split into 2 equal bowls (one for sauce, one for layering).
3
Boil 1 lb cavatappi in heavily salted water ("like the ocean") for 8 min (or 7 min if baking later). Drain well.
4
Make roux: Melt 3 tbsp butter over medium heat. Add half the seasoning blend and bloom 30 sec. Whisk in 3 tbsp flour and cook 2–3 min until bubbly/paste-like.
5
Build sauce: Slowly stream in 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk, whisking 1 min until smooth. Whisk in 1 tbsp Dijon mustard.
6
Slowly pour in 2 cups (1 pint) heavy cream and the remaining seasoning blend. Simmer & whisk ~2 min until slightly thickened.
7
Melt cheese: Turn heat to LOW. Add Bowl #1 of shredded cheese a handful at a time, letting each handful melt fully before adding the next.
8
Fold in cooked cavatappi until every noodle is coated.
9
Layer in dish: Half of mac & cheese → half of Bowl #2 cheese → rest of mac & cheese → remaining cheese on top.
10
Bake 25–30 min at 350°F until bubbly. Broil 2 min to brown top. Garnish with chopped chives.
